Steps 4 & 5 Hand-Off
Phase 4: CUCM Dial-Plan & Smart Licenses Mandatory

To prevent split-brain routing and dropped calls during coexistence, CUCM Translation Patterns must route migrated extensions to the Local Gateway (CUBE/LGW). Physical SEP<MAC> devices must then be disassociated and deleted from CUCM 12.5 to reclaim Cisco Smart Licenses (UCL/CUWL).

Phase 5: Automated 8-Point Cutover Verification Sign-Off

Execute automated post-cutover verification across your newly provisioned Webex Calling workspaces. Evaluates cloud SIP registration status, extension dial-plan parity, RedSky E911 dispatch mapping, and tenant license reconciliation.

Day-2 Operations & Recovery

Correct Workspace Locations & Recover Phones

Use the same CSV format as the workspace import, with the corrected locatioName (or locationId). Preview checks exact workspace matches and assigned MACs before making changes. Non-calling workspaces are moved in place; for Calling-enabled workspaces, the tool creates a replacement at the target location, verifies Calling and MPP assignments, and only then deletes the original. The separate recovery action restores detached phones to their original workspaces without changing locations.
